PM Operating System

Build the products that build the company.

How I actually work — whether I'm building something new or fixing what's already live.

Find the real problem.

I start by listening — to the data, to customers, to the market. The goal is finding the real problem, not the loudest request.

"Most 'asks' aren't the actual problem. My job is finding the one underneath."

Place a clear bet.

Turn the problem into a clear bet — what we're building, why it'll work, and how we'll know if it didn't.

"If I can't explain the bet in two sentences, it's not ready yet."

Ship without the mess.

Cut scope down to what actually matters, keep engineering unblocked, and protect quality the whole way through.

"Speed without a mess — that's the job."

Learn from what ships.

Ship in stages, watch what actually happens, and feed it straight back into what we build next.

"Launch is day one, not the finish line."
